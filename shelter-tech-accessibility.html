Accessibility Audit Driving 100% Issue Resolution on ShelterTech.org

ShelterTech.org is a San Francisco nonprofit that bridges the digital divide through technology. I led accessibility audits for ShelterTech.org and SFServiceGuide.org, then turned the findings into fixes the team could carry forward on its own.

01 — Audit

Finding the gaps

I audited both platforms against WCAG 2.1 A and AA. The audits uncovered failure rates of 37% on ShelterTech.org and 24% on SFServiceGuide.org. I turned the findings into prioritized, actionable fixes.

02 — Enablement

Building capability, not just fixing issues

I created a “How to Test for Accessibility” training deck and a step-by-step checklist, so developers could test and remediate issues on their own. The goal was to strengthen compliance over the long term and build a culture of inclusive design across the team.

Results

What changed

  • ShelterTech.org: reduced the accessibility failure rate from 37% to 0%, with full WCAG 2.1 AA compliance
  • SFServiceGuide.org: identified a 24% failure rate, with all critical and high-severity issues remediated
  • Across both platforms: 0 remaining critical accessibility blockers
  • Tested against 60+ WCAG 2.1 criteria at the A and AA levels
